## Playtest protocol and triage

Recruit people who enjoy fast management games as well as at least one Football Manager player and one newcomer. Give a goal ("pick a club, field a team, play, improve the squad") without navigation coaching. Observe time-to-first-match, clicks needed to compare/sign a player, unclear consequences, unplanned interruptions and voluntary continuation. Record consented notes without collecting unnecessary personal data. No claim that these small samples represent the market.

Critical: lost/corrupt save, repeatable crash or career progression impossible. High: incorrect ownership/money/discipline, broken accessibility path or platform launch failure. Medium: misleading explanation, badly paced interruption or meaningful balance exploit. Low: cosmetic defect. Critical/high block launch. Fix medium issues that defeat the central loop before adding depth. An owner-approved residual issue must name workaround and affected players; no blanket "known issues" exemption.
